How FlintRay works

We ask the AI assistants your buyers use the questions your buyers ask. Then we show you who they recommend, why, and what to change. Every fix is checked on the next run.

Your buyers' real situations

We start from who buys from you. We research your segments and order them by what each is worth to you. Then we write the situations those buyers are in: who they are, where they are, what they need, and what rules options out.

  • Your whole offer

    Situations cover your main service, the services you offer but may not be winning, and the partners who refer buyers to you.

  • National and local

    Local is often where a smaller company can win, so we test both.

  • Your buyers' languages

    Each situation runs in the language your buyers use.

  • Your name stays out

    We never name you in a question. We want to see whether the assistant finds you on its own.

  • Read before it runs

    Someone reads every situation as your team would and asks one question: could you actually win this one?

Three turns in four assistants

People explain their problem to an assistant, add constraints, then ask for names. So every situation is a three-turn conversation, run in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity.

  • The problem

    Who I am, what's happening, and the general question.

  • The constraints

    Budget, timing, location, compliance and language: the things that rule options out.

  • The ask

    "Which specific companies would you contact? I'd like names I can call this week."

The shortlist forms on the third turn. That answer is the one we measure.

What we record for every answer

The same assistant can answer differently on a different model, so the model is part of the record.

  • The answer and its model

    For every answer we record the assistant, the model label the app showed, and the turn. The model is checked before each question and after each answer; an answer given on the wrong model is discarded and never counted.

  • Where it ran

    Conversations run in the same consumer apps buyers use, on paid plans, signed in, in a private session with memory and personalization off.

  • The exact words

    We quote every answer word for word. A company counts only when the assistant's own words name it. If the quote isn't in the answer, it doesn't count.

How each answer is classified

Every company in a third-turn answer gets one of four labels. "Put forward" means recommended or shortlisted.

  • Recommended

    The assistant tells the buyer to contact you.

  • Shortlisted

    You're on the list it suggests comparing.

  • Mentioned

    You're named, but the assistant doesn't put you forward.

  • Not named

    You're absent from the answer.

Only complete conversations count. Every number shows its base, as in "put forward in 7 of 40".

The sources behind each answer

Assistants read pages before they answer. We record which ones: your site, your competitors' sites, directories, review sites and publishers.

You see which pages decided each shortlist, which of them mention you, and where you need to be.

What we see deciding those shortlists

The fix plan

The evidence becomes a plan of specific work, each item with an owner. Your team can do the work, or we can on Lens Engage.

  • A page for each situation you lose

    Assistants answer specific questions with specific pages.

  • Honest comparison pages

    For the names the assistants already trust.

  • One consistent story

    Across your own pages. Assistants repeat contradictions, or skip you.

  • Facts an assistant can read

    Coverage, pricing, integrations and credentials, stated plainly and marked up.

  • Listings and mentions

    On the sites assistants use to build shortlists.

The rerun that checks each fix

We run your situations again every two weeks on Lens Monitor and every week on Lens Engage. One answer is a snapshot. Repeated runs show the trend, and what moved it.

Each rerun shows whether a fix moved the answer. Your team gets a live report with every conversation saved, and you can connect your own AI assistant to the data to ask which situations you lost this week, and to whom.

Questions

Do you name my company in the questions?

No. We want to see whether an assistant finds and recommends you on its own, the way it would for a real buyer.

Which AI assistants do you test?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, in the same consumer apps your buyers use. We record the model behind every answer.

How often do you rerun the questions?

Every two weeks on Lens Monitor and every week on Lens Engage. Reruns show whether a change you made moved the answer.

What counts as being recommended?

The assistant has to tell the buyer to contact you, in its own words, when the buyer asks which companies to contact. We quote it exactly. If the quote isn't in the answer, it doesn't count.

Can my team read the conversations?

Yes. Every conversation is saved in your live report, and you can connect your own AI assistant to the data to ask questions about it.
