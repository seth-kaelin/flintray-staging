How FlintRay works

We run your buyers' conversations through ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity and read every turn, so you can see who the assistants bring up early and who they pick first when the buyer asks for names. Then we check every fix on the next run.

Your buyers' real situations

We start from research into who buys from you, and write the situations those buyers are really in. Before anything runs, a person reads each one the way your team would and asks whether you could actually win it.

Your name never appears in a question. We want to see whether the assistant finds you on its own, the way it would for a real buyer.

Three turns in four assistants

People explain their problem to an assistant, add what rules options out, and only then ask for names. So every situation runs as a three-turn conversation in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. The buyer sets out the problem, then the constraints, then asks which companies they'd contact. Every conversation runs in the assistant's own web app, in a fresh session.

By the time the buyer asks for names, the assistant may already have brought up a few companies. So we track who's named at every turn, and classify the final answer, the one the buyer acts on.

What we record for every answer

The same assistant can answer differently on a different model.

Every answer is recorded with the assistant that gave it, and the exact model wherever the assistant shows one. Each answer keeps its place in the conversation, so you can see when a company first came up.

We quote every answer word for word. A company counts only when the assistant's own words name it.

How each answer is classified

When the buyer asks for names, every company in the answer gets one of five labels. "Put forward" means recommended or shortlisted.

Only complete conversations count. Every number shows its base, as in "put forward in 7 of 40".

The sources behind each answer

Assistants cite pages when they answer, and we record every one, whether it's your site, a competitor's, a directory, a review site or a publisher.

You see which pages each answer cited, which of them mention you, and where you need to be.

What we see deciding those shortlists

The fix plan

The evidence becomes a plan of specific work, each item with an owner, and it goes after the early questions first. Your team does the work on Lens Monitor, and we do it on Lens Engage.

Each item names the page, comparison or listing to change, and the situations it should move.

The rerun that checks each fix

We run your situations again on a set schedule on Lens Monitor and every week on Lens Engage. A single answer is only a snapshot, so it's the repeated runs that show the trend and what moved it.

Each rerun shows whether a fix moved the answer. Your team gets a live report with every conversation saved, and you can connect your own AI assistant to the data to ask which situations you lost this week, and to whom.

Questions

Why measure every turn?

Across 712 buyer conversations, a company that came up in the buyer's first questions and made the final shortlist was the top pick 1.7 times as often as one that only appeared at the shortlist. It's a correlation, so we track every turn and use the next run to check whether early work moved the final answer.

Do you name my company in the questions?

No. We want to see whether an assistant finds and recommends you on its own, the way it would for a real buyer.

Which AI assistants do you test?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. Every answer is tied to the assistant that gave it, with the exact model wherever it's shown.

How often do you rerun the questions?

On a set schedule on Lens Monitor and every week on Lens Engage. Reruns show whether a change you made moved the answer.

What counts as being recommended?

The assistant has to tell the buyer to contact you, in its own words, when the buyer asks which companies to contact. We quote it exactly. If the quote isn't in the answer, it doesn't count.

Can my team read the conversations?

Yes. Every conversation is saved in your live report, and you can connect your own AI assistant to the data to ask questions about it.
