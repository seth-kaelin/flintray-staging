How FlintRay works

We ask the AI assistants your buyers use the questions your buyers ask. Then we show you who they recommend, why, and what to change. Every fix is checked on the next run.

1

Your buyers' real situations

We start from who buys from you, not from keywords. We research your segments and rank them by what they're worth to you. Then we write the situations those buyers are in: who they are, where they are, what they need, and what rules options out.

  • Situations cover your main service, the services you offer but may not be winning, and partners who refer buyers to you.
  • National and local situations, because local is often where a smaller company can win.
  • In the languages your buyers use.
  • We never name you in a question. We want to see whether the AI finds you on its own.
  • Before anything runs, someone reads every situation as your team would and asks: could you actually win this one?
2

Three turns, like a real conversation

People don't ask an assistant one keyword. They explain, add constraints, then ask for names. So every situation is a three-turn conversation:

  1. The problem

    Who I am, what's happening, and the general question.

  2. The constraints

    Budget, timing, location, compliance, language. The things that rule options out.

  3. The ask

    "Which specific companies would you contact? I'd like names I can call this week."

The third turn is where the shortlist forms. It's the answer we measure.

3

Four AIs, the way people use them

Each conversation runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, in the same apps your buyers use. We record the model behind every answer, because the same assistant can answer differently on a different model.

Then we run it again, every week or every two weeks. One answer is a snapshot; repeated runs show you a trend, and what moved it.

4

What we count, and how

Recommended
The AI tells the buyer to contact you.
Shortlisted
You're on the list it suggests comparing.
Mentioned
You're named, but not put forward.
Not named
You're not in the answer at all.

We only count a company when the AI's own words name it, quoted exactly. If the quote isn't in the answer, it doesn't count. Only complete conversations count, and every number shows what it's out of: "put forward in 7 of 40", never a bare percentage.

5

Why each answer happened

Assistants read pages before they answer. We record which ones: your site, your competitors' sites, directories, review sites, publishers. You see which pages decided each shortlist, which of them mention you, and where you need to be. What we see deciding those shortlists.

6

Fixes, checked against the next run

The evidence turns into a plan of specific work, each item with an owner:

  • A page for every situation you're losing. AI answers specific questions with specific pages.
  • Honest comparison pages for the names AI already trusts.
  • One consistent story across your own pages. AI repeats contradictions, or skips you.
  • Facts AI can read: coverage, pricing, integrations and credentials, stated plainly and marked up.
  • Listings and mentions on the sites AI uses to build shortlists.

Your team can do the work, or we can (Lens Engage). Either way, the next run shows whether each fix moved the answer.

7

A live report, and your AI can read it

Your whole team gets a live report with every conversation saved. You can also connect your own AI assistant to the data and ask it questions: which situations did we lose this week, and to whom?

Questions

Do you name my company in the questions?

No. We want to see whether an assistant finds and recommends you on its own, the way it would for a real buyer.

Which AI assistants do you test?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, in the same apps your buyers use. We record the model behind every answer.

How often do you rerun the questions?

Every two weeks on Lens Monitor and every week on Lens Engage. Reruns show whether a change you made moved the answer.

What counts as being recommended?

An assistant has to name you in its own words when the buyer asks which companies to contact. We quote it exactly, and if the quote isn't in the answer, it doesn't count.

Can my team read the conversations?

Yes. Every conversation is saved in your live report, and you can connect your own AI assistant to the data to ask questions about it.
