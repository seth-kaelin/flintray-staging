How much does AEO cost?

What it costs in 2026 to find out where you stand in AI answers and to fix it, and what each price buys.

Guide by FlintRay Research. Updated 2 November 2026. 3 minute read.

When buyers ask an AI assistant for a shortlist before contacting anyone, a company missing from that shortlist may never hear about the deal.

The short answer

AEO (answer engine optimization) is the work of measuring and improving whether AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity recommend your company when buyers ask. Prices fall into three bands:

What sets the price

These are the things that drive the price in our work, and they are good questions to ask any provider.

3 Our own measurement for an anonymized client: two complete runs in October 2026.

  • How many buyer situations are tracked. A situation is a real buyer with real constraints, such as a buyer comparing providers who meet a specific requirement in a specific location. More situations increase what is tracked, and cost more to run.
  • How many AI assistants are checked. They don't agree. On one client's questions, ChatGPT put the company forward in 75 of 148 conversations and Perplexity in 25 of 148.3 Checking one assistant can miss what the others say.
  • How the questions are asked. A buyer explains their problem, adds their constraints, then asks for names. A full conversation like that takes more work to run than a single prompt, and it is closer to what buyers actually do.
  • How often it is rerun. Answers change as assistants and the web change. Weekly or fortnightly reruns cost more than a one-off check, and they are a reliable way to know whether a change you made worked.
  • Who does the fixes. Measurement tells you what to change. Writing and placing pages and listings adds delivery work and cost, whether your team does it or a provider does.

How to decide

Start by finding out whether you have a problem at all. Some companies are already recommended in most of the situations that matter; others are never named. An audit answers that before you commit to a monthly spend.

If you're losing and your team can make the changes, buy measurement and a plan. If nobody on the team owns this, budget for someone to do the work as well. Either way, insist on reruns. Without them you can't tell which change moved the answer, and you will keep paying for work you can't evaluate.
