Privacy

What this website collects, why, who handles it, and how to have it deleted.

Last updated 2 November 2026

Who we are

FlintRay operates this website and is the controller of the personal data it collects. For anything about your data, write to privacy@flintray.com.

What we collect

We collect only what you type into our forms.

  • Audit requests: your company's website, your work email and, if you choose to give it, the competitor you lose to most.
  • Partner inquiries: your firm's website, your work email and, if you choose to give it, how many clients the service might suit.

This site sets no cookies. It runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

Why we use it

We use your details to reply to you and, if you asked for an audit, to prepare it. An audit looks only at public information about your company and at how AI assistants answer your buyers' questions.

We don't sell your details, and we don't add you to a mailing list.

Who else handles it

  • Formspree receives form submissions and forwards them to our mailbox.
  • Google Workspace hosts our email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like most web hosts, it may log visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces in the interactive sample report only. The rest of this site serves its own fonts.

How long we keep it

We keep your message for as long as we need it to reply and follow up. Ask us to delete it at any time and we will.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. Write to privacy@flintray.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
