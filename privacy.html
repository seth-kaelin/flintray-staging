Privacy

What this website collects, why, who handles it, and how to have it deleted.

Last updated 2 November 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by FlintRay. For anything about your data, write to privacy@flintray.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms:

  • Audit requests: your company's website, your work email, and, if you choose, the competitor you lose to most.
  • Partner inquiries: your firm's website, your work email, and, if you choose, how many clients the service might suit.

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

Why we use it

To reply to you, and, if you asked for an audit, to prepare it. An audit looks only at public information about your company and how AI assistants answer your buyers' questions. We don't sell your details, and we don't add you to a mailing list.

Who else handles it

  • Formspree receives form submissions and forwards them to our mailbox.
  • Google Workspace hosts our email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like most web hosts, it may log visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on this site, so your browser connects to Google when a page loads.

How long we keep it

We keep your message for as long as we need it to reply and follow up. Ask us to delete it at any time and we will.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Write to privacy@flintray.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
