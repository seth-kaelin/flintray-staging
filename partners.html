What we do behind it

The research and the delivery are ours, under your name.

  • Research

    We study each client's buyers and write their buyer situations.

  • Conversations

    We run every situation through ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, on schedule.

  • Analysis

    We write the analysis, the report and the fix plan.

  • Fixes

    We make the fixes ourselves, if you want us to.

How delivery starts

It starts with one client or prospect of yours and a free audit in your brand.

  1. A free audit in your brand

    Pick the client or prospect. We run the audit for them and deliver it under your name.

  2. You lead the walkthrough

    You show them which assistants put their competitors forward, and why. We prepare you for the call.

  3. They sign with you

    The client takes monitoring, or monitoring with the fixes done for them. You set the price they pay. We bill you at partner rates.
