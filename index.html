An AI recommendation is word of mouth

When a buyer asks ChatGPT which companies to call, the answer lands the way a tip from a trusted colleague does.

It gives a few names and a reason for each, and the one named first carries the most weight. A company that never gets named doesn't learn the deal existed, and nothing shows up in its analytics.

The decision starts forming in the first question

Buyers usually describe the problem first, then add what rules options out, and only ask for names at the end.

By then the assistant may already have named a few companies. So we measure the whole conversation, every turn, and note who gets named at each step.

Across 712 buyer conversations in four AI assistants, a company that came up in the buyer's first questions and made the final shortlist was the top pick 1.7 times as often as one that only appeared at the shortlist, 113 of 888 against 241 of 3,144. It's a correlation, so we use the next run to check what actually moved.

We work to get you named early

We work on the pages and sources assistants lean on while the buyer is still describing the problem, so you're named and cited in the questions buyers ask before they ask for names. Then the next run shows whether it moved.

  1. Your buyers' situations

    Written from research into who buys from you, and read by a person who asks whether you could actually win each one. Your name never appears in the questions.

  2. Three turns in four assistants

    The buyer sets out the problem, then the constraints, then asks which companies to contact, in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity.

  3. Every turn, read by a person

    We note who's named at each step. In the final answer every company is Recommended, Shortlisted, Mentioned, Named negatively or Not named, and each call rests on an exact quote.

  4. Sources

    Every cited source is sorted by type, whether it's your site, a competitor's, a directory, a review site, a publisher or a forum.

  5. Fixes

    Specific pages, comparisons and listings aimed at the early questions, each with an owner.

  6. Rerun

    The same situations, run again. Each fix is marked moved, no change yet, or worse.
