How it works

  1. 1

    Your buyers' real situations

    We research who buys from you and write the situations they're in, by segment, region and language. Each is a three-turn conversation: the problem, the constraints, then "which companies would you contact?" We never name you in the questions.

  2. 2

    Every major AI, the way buyers use it

    Each conversation runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, in the same apps your buyers use, as a full three-turn conversation. We record the model behind every answer.

  3. 3

    The evidence behind every answer

    Who was recommended, shortlisted or mentioned, quoted word for word, and the pages each AI read before it answered. You see why a competitor won, not just that it did.

  4. 4

    Fixes, checked against the next run

    A plan of specific pages, comparisons and listings, each with an owner. We can do the work for you. Every fix is checked on the next run, so you know what moved the answer.
