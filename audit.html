Your free AI search audit

We ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity the questions your buyers ask. We show you who they put forward, and why. Then we walk you through it on a call.

Request your auditRead the sample report

What the audit includes

Free for companies that fit our scope. We write 15 of your buyers' situations and run each as a three-turn conversation in four assistants: 60 conversations in all. You get a private report and a 30 to 40 minute walkthrough.

  • Your buyers' situations

    Written from research into who buys from you. Each conversation ends with the buyer asking which companies to contact.

  • All four assistants

    ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, in the same consumer apps your buyers use.

  • Who gets put forward

    The companies the assistants put forward in your place, how often, and in which assistant.

  • Every conversation, saved

    Read exactly what each assistant said.

  • The pages behind the answers

    Which sites each assistant read, and which of them mention you.

  • A first plan

    The specific pages and listings most likely to change the answer.

The report arrives as a private, unindexed link. Your team can keep it.

Who it's for

Marketing leads and founders at B2B software and services companies of 20 to 500 people, whose bigger rival gets named by AI when they don't.

The audit is free for companies that fit our scope and aren't direct competitors of a current client. We run one client per competitive set. If we already work with a direct competitor of yours, we'll tell you up front. If you're outside our scope, we'll say so and offer a paid version.

What happens next

  • You ask

    Tell us your website and the competitor you lose to most.

  • We research

    We study your buyers and write the situations. We never name you in the questions.

  • We run it

    Every situation goes through all four assistants, and every answer is checked word for word.

  • We walk you through it

    On the call you see who wins your buyers, why, and what to fix first.

After the audit

If you want to keep going, there are two ongoing services. Both are custom scope.

  • Lens Monitor

    Custom scope, 3-month minimum. Your situations rerun every two weeks, with a live report, the pages behind every answer, and a fix plan your team works through.

  • Lens Engage

    Custom scope, 6-month minimum. Monitor rerun weekly, plus we write and place the fixes and review the results with you each month.

Questions

Is the audit really free?

Yes. There is no charge and no commitment. We walk you through the results on a call and you keep the report.

What do you need from me?

Your website and the competitor you lose to most. We do the research and write the buyer situations ourselves.

Will you contact my customers or competitors?

No. We only ask AI assistants questions and read public pages.

Do you work with my competitors?

We work with one company per competitive set. If we already work with a direct competitor of yours, we'll tell you before we run anything.
