Questions people ask first

What does the audit add to a cheap tracking tool?

Tracking tools run from about $30 to $1,000 a month on published plans, and they'll tell you how often your name comes up. The audit runs your real buyer situations as full conversations, and a person reads every turn exactly as it was given, so you see where the decision started to form. You also see the sources behind each answer and the first fixes to make.

We already use a tracker. Do we still need this?

Keep it. Your tracker tells you when your numbers move, and the audit shows you why they move, with the answers and the sources behind them.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. We're selective, and we take B2B software and services companies with 20 to 500 people. You keep the report. It covers 15 of your buyers' situations in all four assistants, which is one real slice of your market, and the paid work covers the rest.

Do you work with my competitors?

Free audits are open to every company, your rivals included. On Lens Engage, you name 3 to 5 rivals, and none of them can become a paying client while you're on Engage. We never use one client's data to pitch another company.

After the audit

If you want the full picture and the fixes, there are two ways to keep going, both monthly.

  • Lens Monitor

    We measure and your team fixes. Your situations rerun on a set schedule, with a live report, the pages behind every answer, and a fix plan your team works through.

    Both plans are monthly. Lens Monitor is scoped on the call.

  • Lens Engage

    We measure and we do the fixes. Your situations rerun every week, we write and place the fixes, and we review the results with you each month. You name 3 to 5 rivals, and none of them can become a paying client while you're on Engage. Your first month includes the full audit and the first fixes.

    Lens Engage starts at $5,000 a month.

How we make fixes

When we make the fixes on Lens Engage, we never post under fake names, buy reviews or invent quotes.
