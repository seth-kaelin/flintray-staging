What the audit includes

We write 15 of your buyers' situations and run each one as a three-turn conversation in all four assistants, about 60 conversations in all. Each one runs the way a real buyer's does, from the problem to the shortlist. We read every turn, so you'll see who the assistants bring up early as well as who they pick first.

  • Your buyers' situations

    Written from research into who buys from you. Each conversation ends with the buyer asking which companies to contact.

  • All four assistants

    ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. Every answer is tied to the exact model that gave it.

  • Who gets put forward

    The companies the assistants put forward in your place, how often, and in which assistant.

  • Every conversation, saved

    Read exactly what each assistant said, turn by turn.

  • The pages behind the answers

    Which sites each assistant read, and which of them mention you.

  • A first plan

    The specific pages and listings to change first.

The report arrives as a private, unindexed link, and your team can keep it.

Questions people ask first

What does the audit add to a cheap tracking tool?

Tracking tools run from about $30 to $1,000 a month on published plans, and they'll tell you how often your name comes up. The audit runs your real buyer situations as full conversations, and a person reads every turn exactly as it was given, so you see where the decision started to form. You also see the sources behind each answer and the first fixes to make.

We already use a tracker. Do we still need this?

Keep it. Your tracker tells you when your numbers move, and the audit shows you why they move, with the answers and the sources behind them.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. We're selective, and we take B2B software and services companies with 20 to 500 people. You keep the report. It covers 15 of your buyers' situations in all four assistants, which is one real slice of your market, and the paid work covers the rest.

Who is it for?

Marketing leads and founders at B2B software and services companies of 20 to 500 people, whose bigger rival gets named by AI when they don't. If you're outside that, we'll say so when we reply.

What do you need from me?

Your website and the competitor you lose to most. We do the research and write the buyer situations ourselves.

Will you contact my customers or competitors?

No. We only ask AI assistants questions and read public pages.

After the audit

If you want the full picture and the fixes, there are three ways to keep going. Each one is scoped to your market.

  • Audit and fix

    A one-time project, custom scope. We run your full set of buyer situations, write the fix plan, make the fixes, and rerun the same situations to prove them.

  • Lens Monitor

    Custom scope, 3-month minimum. We measure and your team fixes. Your situations rerun every two weeks, with a live report, the pages behind every answer, and a fix plan your team works through.

  • Lens Engage

    Custom scope, 6-month minimum. We measure and we do the fixes. Your situations rerun every week, we write and place the fixes, and we review the results with you each month.

  • How we make fixes

    When we make the fixes, in Audit and fix or Lens Engage, we never post under fake names, buy reviews or invent quotes.
