Fixes

  1. Do this first

    Answer buyers’ exact questions on your own site

    Owner
    Marketing
    Effort
    Weeks

    The 6 conversations that cited Corvane's own pages are the 6 that put it forward, so more of those pages is the first thing to test on the next run.

    • A page per buying question: the SOC 2 timeline, Type I versus Type II, ISO 27001 alongside SOC 2, what an audit costs.
    • Prices and plan limits written as text on the page.
    • A readiness guide for every framework you support.

  2. Do this next

    Get onto the review sites and guides AI cites

    Owner
    Marketing
    Effort
    Weeks

    The assistants cited third-party sites 201 times, against 32 for vendors' own sites.

    • Complete, current profiles on the review sites AI cited.
    • Inclusion in “best compliance software” guides for mid-market companies.
    • A steady flow of recent customer reviews from security and IT leads.

  3. Do this next

    Make your facts consistent everywhere

    Owner
    Marketing
    Effort
    Days

    51 of the 60 final answers brought up price and 36 brought up which frameworks a tool covers, so those are the facts to keep the same on every page and profile.

    • One set of facts (frameworks supported, time to audit readiness, price) on every page and profile.
    • Remove outdated offers and dates.
