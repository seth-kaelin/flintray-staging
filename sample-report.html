Method

Every number on this page is counted from the audit’s own records.

Outcome
A company’s strongest role in the final turn: Recommended, Shortlisted, Mentioned or Named negatively. Named only in earlier turns counts as Mentioned. Never named counts as Not named.
Put forward
Recommended or Shortlisted in the final turn.
Top pick
The first company Recommended in the final turn, or the first Shortlisted when none was Recommended.
Source type
A rival’s own site is one the audit marks as that rival’s. Directories, publishers and forums follow the audit’s site types plus a short list of known sites. Everything else counts as Other.
Anonymized
Rivals are lettered from A by how often they were put forward. Situations are numbered in audit order. Names, sites and questions are left out.
